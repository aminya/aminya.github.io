Office Abacus AI for Microsoft 365

Office Abacus AI is an AI assistant embedded in Microsoft 365. I led the whole project across Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and Outlook, developing workflows that let users chat with their current file, summarize and analyze documents, generate reports and charts, create and edit visual assets, and automate repetitive formatting and spreadsheet work without leaving the Office application.

Key capabilities include:

  • Contextual AI chat grounded in the active document, workbook, presentation, or email
  • Excel table analysis, formula assistance, cleanup, report generation, and chart creation
  • Document analysis, spreadsheet generation, image analysis, and image generation
  • Formatting assistance for polished tables and documents
  • Microsoft Teams integration for collaborative workflows
