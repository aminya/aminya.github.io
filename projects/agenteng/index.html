Founding Lead AI Engineer at AgentEng



As the founding Lead AI Engineer at AgentEng, I developed SPARC—the first AI agent for PSCAD and power-system software. SPARC gives electrical engineers an agentic workflow for automating studies, tuning systems, and optimizing engineering objectives.

SPARC and the agentic workflow

  • Developed the SPARC AI agent for PSCAD, helping electrical engineers optimize power systems through an agentic workflow
  • Built the Rust/Tauri agent client and its integration with power-system engineering workflows
  • Developed a touch-enabled objective-drawing system that lets engineers draw their desired target outcomes directly on a surface

AI optimization and platform engineering

  • Implemented a proprietary multi-objective optimization algorithm that uses AI to optimize power-system behavior against engineer-defined targets
  • Developed the AI/LLM backend with LangGraph and the Claude API
  • Built the Bun/Elysia server that coordinates communication between the agent client and LLM backends

SPARC demonstration

The project combines engineering-domain workflows with modern AI infrastructure to make complex power-system studies more interactive, explainable, and efficient. Learn more at the AgentEng website.
